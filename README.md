Multi-Agent Generative AI Stock Market Simulation

Overview
This project is a Multi-Agent Generative AI Stock Market Simulation designed to model realistic financial market behavior using autonomous AI trading agents. The system simulates a virtual stock market with two tradable stocks, Stock A and Stock B, over a complete 264-day trading cycle. AI-powered agents independently make buy, sell, or hold decisions based on market conditions, risk appetite, trading strategy, news events, and capital availability.


Objective

The project aims to study emergent market behavior created through interactions between heterogeneous AI agents. It demonstrates how autonomous decision-making can lead to realistic market trends, volatility, bubbles, crashes, and liquidity fluctuations.


Key Features


Two dynamically priced stocks

50 autonomous AI trading agents

Generative AI-based trading decisions

Multiple trading strategies and risk profiles

News and sentiment-driven market events

Loan, leverage, and interest mechanisms

Bankruptcy detection and market exit system

Market-wide analytics and performance tracking
